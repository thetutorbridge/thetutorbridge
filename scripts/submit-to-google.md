# Google Search Console - Rapid Indexing Guide

## ✅ Step 1: Submit Your Sitemap (DO THIS FIRST)

1. Go to **Google Search Console**: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Select your property: `thetutorbridge.com`
3. Go to **Sitemaps** (left sidebar)
4. Add your sitemap URL: `https://www.thetutorbridge.com/sitemap.xml`
5. Click **Submit**

**Expected Result:** Google will start crawling all 9,300+ pages automatically.

**How to Request Indexing:**
1. In Google Search Console, go to **URL Inspection** tool
2. Paste the URL
3. Click **Request Indexing**
4. Wait 1-2 minutes
5. Repeat for each URL (limit: ~10-20 per day)

### Check Coverage:
1. Go to **Coverage** report in Google Search Console
2. Look for:

   - ⚠️ Valid with warnings
   - ❌ Errors (fix these!)
   - 📊 Excluded (usually fine)

## 🔥 Step 4: Speed Up Indexing with Internal Links

**Why this works:** Google crawls your site faster when pages are well-linked.

### Add "Related Content" to Every Page:
- Link word problems to related formulas
- Link formulas to related geometry problems
- Link calculators to related word problems
- Cross-link between categories

**Example:**
On a geometry page, add:
- "Related: [Area of Triangle Word Problem]"
- "Related: [Area Formula Reference]"
- "Related: [Triangle Calculator]"
