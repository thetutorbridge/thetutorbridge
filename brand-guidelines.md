# TheTutorBridge Brand Guidelines

## Brand Personality

- **Tone**: Balanced (Trustworthy for parents, Energetic for students)
- **Keywords**: Guidance, Growth, Pathways, Vision, Support
- **Feel**: Inspirational + Professional + Friendly

---

## Color Palette

We use a **3-color system** for consistency:

- **Primary Color (Trust)**: Deep Blue (#1A3D7C)
    → Represents reliability, education, and mentorship.
    
- **Secondary Color (Growth)**: Teal Green (#2BAE66)
    → Represents progress, success, and balance.
    
- **Accent Color (Energy)**: Warm Yellow/Amber (#FFC857)
    → Represents curiosity, creativity, and youthfulness.
    
- **Neutral Shades**:
    - Light Gray (#F5F5F5) for backgrounds
    - Dark Charcoal (#2C2C2C) for text
    - Pure White (#FFFFFF) for clean backgrounds

---

## Typography

Balanced between professional + playful:

- **Primary Font**: *Poppins* (sans-serif) → modern, friendly, readable for students
- **Secondary Font**: *Merriweather* (serif) → authority, trust, and academic depth

Usage:

- Headings → **Poppins Bold**
- Body → **Merriweather Regular**
- Highlights/Quotes → *Poppins Medium Italic*

---

## Buttons

### Primary Button

- Background: Deep Blue (#1A3D7C)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Shape: Rounded corners (12px radius)
- Hover: Slight shadow + teal glow

**Example Use**: "Book a Free Session" / "Start Your Journey"

### Secondary Button

- Background: Transparent
- Border: 2px Teal Green (#2BAE66)
- Text: Teal Green (#2BAE66)
- Shape: Rounded corners (12px radius)
- Hover: Light teal fill + white text

**Example Use**: "Learn More" / "Explore Courses"

---

## Logo Direction

- **Style**: Clean & Subtle (minimalist design)
- **Symbol**: A **bridge icon** that transitions into an **open book or upward arrow** → symbolizing connection + pathways + growth.
- **Colors**: Blue + Teal base, with Yellow accent highlight.
- **Typography in Logo**: Use *Poppins Semi-Bold* for "The Tutor Bridge" (modern but approachable).

---

## Imagery & Icons

- **Style**: Flat/illustrative icons with soft edges → playful yet professional.
- **Imagery**:
    - Students exploring pathways (bridge, compass, stairs, light bulb)
    - Mentors guiding with care (teacher-student collaborative visuals)
    - Career growth metaphors (arrows, roads, trees with branches = opportunities)

---

## Voice & Messaging

- **For Parents**: "Trusted mentorship that gives your child clarity and confidence."
- **For Students**: "Discover your path. Learn smarter. Dream bigger."
- **Tagline**: *"Guiding Students, Building Futures."*

---

## Applications

- **Website**: Clean sections with vibrant highlights.
- **Social Media**: Motivational quotes + career insights in teal/yellow over white backgrounds.
- **Offline**: Business cards, posters, banners using the 3-color system consistently.

---

## Blog System Branding

### Featured Images
- Use brand colors (Deep Blue #1A3D7C, Teal Green #2BAE66, Warm Yellow #FFC857)
- Professional, clean design style
- Custom illustrations that match blog topic/title
- Images should be **filled** (not fit) in containers
- Each blog must have a distinct, topic-relevant image

### Content Formatting
- Clear distinctions between H2, H3, H4 headings
- Proper link styling with brand colors
- Bold and italic text following brand guidelines
- Numbered lists and bullets with consistent styling
- No table of contents in blog posts
- SEO-optimized meta titles without "| IDP USA" suffix
