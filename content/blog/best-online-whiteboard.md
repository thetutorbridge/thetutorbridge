## Why Online Whiteboards Matter in 2026

There's something powerful about sketching ideas on a whiteboard. The act of visually mapping thoughts, connecting concepts with arrows, and clustering sticky notes helps our brains process information differently than typing in a document ever could.

But physical whiteboards have a problem: they don't work when your team is spread across time zones, your students are learning from home, or your clients are on another continent.

Enter online whiteboards — digital canvases that capture everything valuable about physical whiteboards while adding capabilities that analog boards simply can't match: infinite space, real-time collaboration across continents, AI-powered organization, and persistent boards you can return to months later.

The market agrees. The collaborative whiteboard software market reached **$3.81 billion in 2026** and is projected to hit **$9.59 billion by 2031**, growing at a **20.28% CAGR**. Over **68% of global organizations** now use at least one virtual whiteboard tool for remote workflows.

But with dozens of options available — from enterprise giants like Miro to scrappy upstarts like Excalidraw — choosing the right tool is overwhelming. This guide cuts through the noise with hands-on testing and honest recommendations.

### Is Miro or FigJam better?

### What's the best online whiteboard for teaching?

**Ziteboard** is specifically designed for education with built-in video chat, making it perfect for tutoring. **Microsoft Whiteboard** works well for schools using Microsoft 365. For recorded lessons, **Explain Everything** excels at creating tutorial videos with whiteboard annotation.

### Can I use online whiteboards for free?

Yes. **Canva Whiteboard**, **Excalidraw**, and **Microsoft Whiteboard** offer generous free tiers. Miro, FigJam, and Mural offer limited free plans (typically 3 boards). Most free plans are sufficient for individuals and small teams with basic needs.

### What's the best Miro alternative?

For design teams: **FigJam** ($5/mo, Figma integration). For enterprise workshops: **Mural** ($12/mo, facilitation features). For value: **Lucidspark** ($7.95/mo, Lucidchart integration). For free: **Excalidraw** (open source) or **Canva Whiteboard**.

### Do online whiteboards have AI features?
