# **How can students score more than 95% in Class 10 board exams?**

To score above 95% in Class 10 board exams, students need a strategic study schedule, thorough understanding of NCERT textbooks, regular revision, and smart time management. Focus on important topics, write self-made notes, practice sample papers daily, and work on presentation skills for written answers. This guide offers a subject-wise plan, expert tips, and exam-day strategies to help Class 10 students boost their scores and achieve academic excellence.

Scoring more than 95% in your Class 10 board exams may seem challenging, but you can achieve this milestone with the right approach and dedication. This blog provides you with a step-by-step strategy, subject-wise tips, and the importance of Previous Years' Questions (PYQs) to help you excel.

## **Why Scoring 95% in Class 10 is Achievable**

Class 10 marks are crucial as they set the tone for your future academic and career path. Scoring well opens doors to your desired streams and schools. You can turn this aspiration into reality with effective planning and proper resources.

## **Step-by-Step Guide to Score More Than 95%+**

### **1. Create a Timetable and Stick to It**

- Allocate specific hours for each subject daily.
- Focus more time on weaker areas.
- Include short breaks to maintain focus.

### **2. Prioritize NCERT Books**

NCERT textbooks form the foundation of your preparation. They are mandatory for understanding concepts and taking exams.

### **3. Focus on PYQs (Previous Years' Questions)**

**Why PYQs are Important**:

- Help you understand the exam pattern.
- Highlight frequently asked questions.
- Build familiarity with the marking scheme.

Solve at least the last 5 years' question papers for every subject.

### **4. Take Mock Tests Regularly**

- Simulate exam conditions to practice time management.
- Identify your strengths and weaknesses.
- Use online platforms or offline resources for additional test papers.

### **5. Revise Regularly**

- Dedicate the last 1-2 months entirely to revision.
- Use flashcards, summary notes, and mind maps for quick reviews.

## **Subject-Wise Preparation Tips**

### **Mathematics**

- Focus Areas: Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, and Statistics.

Key Tips:

- Solve all NCERT exercises, including examples and miscellaneous problems.
- Create a formula chart and revise it daily.
- Solve PYQs to understand recurring problem types.

### **Science**

#### **Physics**

- Focus on concepts like Electricity, Light, and Magnetism.
- Practice numerical problems daily and revise derivations.

#### **Chemistry**

- Memorize key chemical reactions and equations.
- Prioritize high-weightage chapters like Carbon Compounds and Periodic Classification.

#### **Biology**

- Learn diagrams thoroughly (e.g., heart, digestive system).
- Write answers in points and include labeled diagrams for better presentation.

### **Social Science**

- **History**: Create timelines for better retention of dates and events.
- **Geography**: Practice maps and understand resource distribution.
- **Civics**: Focus on Power Sharing and Federalism.
- **Economics**: Use real-life examples to enhance your answers.
- Solve PYQs to identify commonly repeated questions in this subject.

### **English**

- Focus Areas: Writing skills, grammar, and literature.

Key Tips:

- Practice letter writing, essays, and precis daily.
- Learn character sketches and themes from literature chapters.
- Solve PYQs for unseen passages and grammar questions.

## **Importance of Previous Years' Questions (PYQs)**

PYQs are a treasure trove of information for students preparing for Class 10 board exams. Here’s why you should prioritize them in your preparation:

- **Identify Exam Trends**: PYQs reveal the type of questions that are frequently asked.\n
- **Boost Confidence**: Familiarity with past papers makes you comfortable with the exam format.\n
- **Target High-Weightage Topics**: PYQs help you focus on important chapters and topics.\n
- **Practice Time Management**: Solving PYQs improves your speed and accuracy, ensuring you finish the paper on time.

## **Last 30-Day Revision Plan**

**Day 1-15**:

- Revise each subject thoroughly, focusing on high-weightage topics.\n
- Solve PYQs chapter-wise.\n

**Day 16-25**:

- Take full-length mock tests and analyze your performance.\n
- Revise weak areas based on test results.\n

**Day 26-30**:

- Revise summary notes and formula sheets.\n
- Solve 2-3 sample papers daily, focusing on speed and accuracy.

## **Exam Day Strategy**

- **Read the Question Paper**: Spend the first 15 minutes understanding the questions and planning your approach.\n
- **Time Management**: Allocate time to each section and stick to it strictly.\n
- **Presentation Matters**: Write neatly, underline keywords, and include diagrams or flowcharts wherever necessary.
