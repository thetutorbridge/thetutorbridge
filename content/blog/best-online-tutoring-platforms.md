## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) {#faqs}

### General Questions

**Q: Are online tutoring platforms worth it?**

Yes, if you choose the right one for your needs. Research shows students using quality online tutoring improve grades by an average of 15-20%. The key is consistency - one session won't transform your learning, but regular sessions with a good tutor will.

**Q: How much does online tutoring cost on average?**

Prices range from free (Khan Academy, library-sponsored Brainfuse) to $100+/hour for premium services. The average cost across platforms is $30-50/hour. Budget options like TutorEye offer live tutoring from $7.49/hour.

**Q: Is online tutoring as effective as in-person?**

Studies show online tutoring is equally effective for most subjects and age groups. For younger children (under 8), in-person may have slight advantages for engagement. For older students, online often works better due to convenience and access to more tutors.

**Q: Can online tutoring help with test preparation?**

Absolutely. Platforms like Revolution Prep specialize in SAT/ACT prep with proven score improvements. Many students see 100-200 point improvements with dedicated test prep tutoring.

### Platform-Specific Questions

**Q: Which platform is best for math tutoring?**

For specialized math tutoring, **Learner.com** offers the best experience. For budget-friendly math help, combine free [calculators](/calculators) and [times tables practice](/times-tables) with affordable platforms like TutorEye.

**Q: What's the best platform for learning languages?**

**Preply** offers the best balance of quality and price for language learning. For budget learners, **iTalki** provides access to affordable native speakers. For English-only conversation practice, **Cambly** is excellent.

**Q: Which platform offers 24/7 availability?**

**Tutor.com**, **TutorMe**, and **StudyGate** all offer 24/7 tutoring. I tested Tutor.com at 3 AM and connected within 5 minutes.

**Q: Are there free online tutoring options?**

Yes! **Khan Academy** is completely free for self-study. **Brainfuse** is free through participating libraries. You can also use free learning tools like [brain games](/brain-games) and [study resources](/study-resources) to supplement your learning.

### Safety and Quality Questions

**Q: How do I know if a tutor is qualified?**

Look for platforms that verify credentials (Varsity Tutors only accepts 10% of applicants). Check tutor reviews, request trial sessions, and don't hesitate to switch tutors if the fit isn't right.

**Q: Is my child safe during online tutoring sessions?**

Reputable platforms include safety features: recorded sessions, moderated chat, and strict tutor background checks. Always choose platforms with clear safety policies and stay aware of your child's sessions.

**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with a session?**

Most platforms offer satisfaction guarantees. Wyzant has a "Good Fit Guarantee," Learner.com offers 100% satisfaction guarantee, and many platforms provide refunds for poor experiences.
