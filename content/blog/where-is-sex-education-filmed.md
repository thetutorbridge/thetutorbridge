## The Real-Life Locations Behind *Sex Education*: A Cinematic Tour 🎥🌲

One of the most fascinating parts of *Sex Education* is how the setting feels like it’s both British and American — at the same time. From riverside chalets to lush valleys and retro-style schools, every scene is packed with natural beauty and production magic.

But where exactly were these scenes shot?

Here’s your **filming location breakdown** — starting with the most iconic place of all:

### 📍 1. Wye Valley, Monmouthshire (Otis’s Chalet)

![Wye Valley, Monmouthshire (Otis’s Chalet)](/blog-images/bdec6dc7659d95d2f26a295d380beecc.jpeg)

Welcome to Otis’s house — the heart of the show and the backdrop for countless emotional moments. This wooden chalet perched on a hill actually exists in **Symonds Yat, Wye Valley**, right along the border of England and Wales.

It overlooks the River Wye and is even available for vacation rental — yes, you can stay there!

### 📍 2. Moordale Secondary School – Caerleon Campus, South Wales

![Moordale Secondary School – Caerleon Campus, South Wales](/blog-images/7870cb3c96d6ea963337397bb3a1f33f.webp)

The fictional **Moordale High School** might remind you of a typical American high school, but it was actually filmed at the **former University of South Wales campus in Caerleon**.

Its red brick walls, gothic windows, and vintage corridors made it the perfect filming set — even though the school doesn’t exist in real life.

> Tip: Students interested in film studies or architecture may want to explore how historical university buildings get repurposed for cinema.

### 📍 3. Penarth, Cardiff, and Newport (Season 3 & 4 Additions)

![Penarth, Cardiff, and Newport (Season 3 & 4 Additions)](/blog-images/a3691cd0ff4fe3fc8f68afa7659696bc.webp)

In later seasons, the show moved to:

- **The Paget Rooms in Penarth** – for school assembly scenes
- **St. Fagans National Museum in Cardiff** – to represent Cavendish Sixth Form
- **Newport International Sports Village** – used for various student activity scenes

These areas added a modern, vibrant twist to the show’s overall tone.

## Inside the Show’s World: Wallace University and More Hidden Filming Gems 🎓🏞️

As the series progressed, *Sex Education* introduced even more dazzling backdrops — especially in Season 4. A big highlight? The mysterious **Wallace University**, where Otis, Maeve, and other students start a new chapter.

Let’s explore where those scenes were actually shot:

### 📍 4. Wallace University – University of Bristol, Clifton Campus

![Wallace University – University of Bristol, Clifton Campus](/blog-images/2f5f4601a64707a984d23fdf88610990.jpeg)

You might recognize the arched libraries, ivy-covered walls, and prestigious vibes of **Wallace University**. But it’s not in the U.S. or even a real place called “Wallace.” In reality, these scenes were filmed at the **University of Bristol’s Clifton Campus**.

- The **Wills Memorial Building** was featured in several interior and exterior scenes.
- Nearby **Royal Fort Gardens** provided some of the peaceful, open-air shots.

### 📍 5. Eric and Otis’s Bridge – Bigsweir Bridge

![Eric and Otis’s Bridge – Bigsweir Bridge](/blog-images/ea63bd34ee96976283cc77b566a091c5.jpeg)

If you remember those heartwarming conversations between Otis and Eric while biking home, they were filmed on the striking **Bigsweir Bridge**, which crosses the River Wye.

This Grade II-listed bridge connects **Gloucestershire and Monmouthshire** and is one of the most Instagram-worthy places in the show.

> Fun Fact: This bridge became so popular after the show aired that it led to a small tourism boom in the Wye Valley!

### 📍 6. Maeve’s Caravan Park – Sterrett’s Caravan Park, Symonds Yat

![Maeve’s Caravan Park – Sterrett’s Caravan Park, Symonds Yat](/blog-images/48516536290e0c05e326deb18542fdd5.jpg)

Maeve Wiley’s tough exterior and private lifestyle are reflected perfectly in her humble caravan home. This location was shot at **Sterrett’s Caravan Park**, just minutes from Otis’s chalet in **Symonds Yat East**.

It’s a working caravan site that fans can visit or even stay at for a full-on *Sex Education* location tour.

## Frequently Asked Questions About *Sex Education* Filming Locations

### ❓Where are the filming locations for *Sex Education*?

*Sex Education* was filmed primarily in the **Wye Valley**, **Monmouthshire**, and surrounding areas in **South Wales and West England**. Major spots include:

- **Otis’s Chalet** – Symonds Yat, Wye Valley
- **Moordale School** – Caerleon Campus, South Wales
- **Wallace University** – University of Bristol
- **Cavendish College** – Penarth and Cardiff

You can see the full list and travel tips [above in this blog](#the-real-life-locations-behind-sex-education-a-cinematic-tour-%F0%9F%8E%A5%F0%9F%8C%B2).

### ❓Was *Sex Education* shot in Nigeria?

No, *Sex Education* was **not filmed in Nigeria**. Despite its diverse cast and globally relatable themes, all known filming locations are in the **UK**, especially **Wales** and **England**.

### ❓Where is the university in *Sex Education* located?

The fictional **Wallace University** scenes were filmed at the **University of Bristol**, primarily at the **Clifton Campus**. The iconic Wills Memorial Building is seen in several interior and exterior shots.

Moordale Secondary School looks like a private school with its **retro American vibe**, lockers, and cafeteria scenes — but it's meant to be a **fictional UK comprehensive school**. The show blends UK and US styles to appeal to a global audience.
