# **How can you calculate percentage easily?**

To calculate a percentage, divide the part by the whole and then multiply the result by 100. The formula is:  
**Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100**.  
For example, if you scored 45 out of 60 marks, the percentage would be (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%.
