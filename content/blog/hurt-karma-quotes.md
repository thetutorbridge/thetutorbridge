### The Psychology Behind Karma & Emotional Release

When emotions run high, especially in social settings like school, students need psychological tools to regulate anger, disappointment, and grief. Karma-centered quotes act as:

- ✨ Verbal affirmations that reframe pain with purpose
- 🧠 Cognitive anchors that reduce rumination and obsessive thoughts
- 🧘 Self-soothing scripts that validate your experience without glorifying revenge

According to cognitive behavioral psychology, writing or repeating truths like “Karma never forgets” or “What you do to others comes back around” gives students clarity and distance from emotional triggers. It’s a grounding mechanism for teens overwhelmed by peer judgment.

Even neuroscience supports it—repeating positive mantras or quotes triggers the brain’s prefrontal cortex, which is responsible for rational thinking, and decreases activity in the amygdala, which controls fear and anger.
